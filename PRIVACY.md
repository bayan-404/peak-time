# Streakline Privacy Policy

_Last updated November 11, 2026_

Streakline helps you plan a "Day N of X" posting series and schedule it to your Buffer account. We keep as little data as possible, and we have no database.

## What we access

When you connect Buffer, you grant Streakline these permissions:

- **account:read** to find your organization and connected channels.
- **posts:write** to create the scheduled posts for your series.
- **posts:read** to show whether each day was posted, upcoming, or missed.

We do not read your analytics, messages, or anything outside these permissions.

## What we store, and where

- **Buffer access tokens** are kept in an encrypted, HTTP-only cookie in your browser. Scripts on the page cannot read it, and we do not keep a copy on our servers.
- **Your series** (name, schedule, daily notes, and the IDs of the posts we created) are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not uploaded to us.
- **Post content** is sent to Buffer only when you click "Schedule". From then on, Buffer's own privacy policy applies to it.

## What we don't do

- No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
- No advertising, tracking, or analytics tools.
- No posting without your click. We only schedule what you review and confirm.

Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and request time) for security and reliability.

## Your control

- **Disconnect** in Streakline deletes the token cookie right away.
- You can also revoke Streakline's access at any time in your Buffer account settings.
- Deleting a series, or clearing your browser data, removes it from your device.
- Posts already scheduled in Buffer stay there until you edit or delete them in Buffer.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Questions? Open an issue at https://github.com/bayan-404/streakline/issues.
